Persona 4 is a role-playing and social simulation video game developed by Atlus. Set in the quiet, rural Japanese town of Inaba, the story follows a high school transfer student and his friends as they investigate a bizarre string of murders tied to a supernatural television dimension known as the "Midnight Channel". The gameplay cleverly splits time between building relationships and managing a normal teenage life during the day, and entering the TV world to fight monstrous "Shadows" using physical manifestations of their psyches, called Personas.
