Characters

This is the protagonist. His official name is weird, but most fans call him "Ren Amamiya".

The protagonist is a second-year high school student who transfers to Shujin Academy in Tokyo, after a false assault charge issued by a high-profile politician, which caused him to end up with a one-year probation sentence. Prior to the incident, it is stated that he lived in a rural area. He lives in a coffee shop called Café Leblanc, located in Yongen-Jaya and owned by Sojiro Sakura, an acquaintance of his parents during his probation.

However, during his first day at his new school, he accidentally enters the palace of his gym teacher, Kamoshida. A Palace is a manifestation of "distortion," strong negative or corrupt thoughts that warp the perceptions of people into a hazard for themselves and others (for example, seeing all other people as living ATMs). According to Morgana, most people's negative and corrupt thoughts are blended together into the Metaverse in a location called Mementos, which is a Palace for all of humanity, as this game's iteration of the Collective Unconscious.

The protagonist ends up taking on the codename "Joker" after he awakens to his persona, "Arsene", after being locked away by Shadow Kamoshida.

He is a being based off the main character of Maurice Leblanc's stories, Arsène Lupin. He appears everywhere and is a master of disguise. Arsene is known as a persona.
